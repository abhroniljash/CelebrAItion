## 🎙️ Scene-by-Scene Script & Walkthrough

### Scene 1: Introduction & App Overview (0:00 – 0:30)
> **Presenter / Voiceover:**
> *"Welcome to CelebrAItion! Planning major milestones—whether it's an intimate vineyard wedding, a high-voltage 30th birthday bash, or a 350-person neighborhood harvest festival—traditionally requires juggling fragmented spreadsheets, scattered email threads, and overwhelming checklists.
> 
> CelebrAItion solves this by uniting a real-time event management suite with generative AI intelligence. Right out of the box, it comes pre-loaded with three realistic, full-scale sample celebrations so you can explore immediately without having to configure a thing."*

**What to show on screen:**
1. Open `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Point out the top header: Days countdown badge, Event Switcher dropdown (`Aria & Julian's Coastal Olive Grove Wedding`, `Leo's Retro Synthwave Birthday`, `Oakridge Harvest Festival`).
3. Point out the 5-tone color harmony swatches and click one to demonstrate the 1-click clipboard copy with green checkmark toast.

---

### Scene 2: 4-Step GenAI Event Wizard (0:30 – 1:15)
> **Presenter / Voiceover:**
> *"Now let's see how easy it is to plan a brand-new celebration from scratch using the AI Wizard.
> 
> In the top right, we click '+ New Event'. In Step 1, we choose our event type—let's select Birthday Celebration. In Step 2, we define our scale: 50 guests and a budget ceiling of \$5,500. In Step 3, we describe our vibe—say, 'Retro 80s Cyberpunk Neon Arcade'. 
> 
> We hit 'Generate Master Blueprint with AI'. In just two seconds, our GenAI engine synthesizes a cohesive 5-tone color harmony, distributes the budget proportionally across venue, drinks, music, and contingency, builds a day-of schedule, and curates bespoke creative ideas with celebration confetti!"*

**What to show on screen:**
1. Click `+ New Event`.
2. Walk through Step 1 (Title: *Max's Neon 30th*), Step 2 (Guest count slider to 50, Budget: $5,500), Step 3 (Select vibe preset *Cyberpunk 80s Neon Arcade*).
3. Hit `Generate Master Blueprint with AI`, watch the progress phases, and click `Adopt & Open Master Plan`.

---

### Scene 3: Budget & Expense Tracker (1:15 – 1:55)
> **Presenter / Voiceover:**
> *"Next, let's explore the Budget & Expense Tracker.
> 
> The top financial cards give you an instant snapshot: Total Target Budget, Committed Expenses, Actual Paid to Date, and your Remaining Safety Buffer.
> 
> Below that, an interactive category breakdown visually shows how your funds are distributed across Venue, Catering, Photography, Music, and Contingency Reserves. You can add custom expenses, mark deposits paid with a single click, filter by priority—essential, recommended, or optional—and export your entire budget to CSV with one click."*

**What to show on screen:**
1. Navigate to the `Budget & Expenses` tab.
2. Show the category distribution progress bars.
3. Click `Add Expense`, add a $400 dessert bar, click save.
4. Click the checkmark icon to mark an item as `Paid`.
5. Click `Export CSV` to show the instant download.

---

### Scene 4: Interactive Timeline & Day-Of Run-of-Show (1:55 – 2:35)
> **Presenter / Voiceover:**
> *"A successful event hinges on timing. CelebrAItion features a dual-mode timeline:
> 
> 1. The Planning Roadmap guides you through key milestones months and weeks prior—like permit filings and catering tastings.
> 2. The Day-Of Run-of-Show breaks your celebration down into an hourly itinerary, from 9:00 AM hair and makeup to the midnight send-off.
> 
> When you mark milestones complete, celebratory confetti triggers, keeping planning rewarding and motivating."*

**What to show on screen:**
1. Navigate to `Timeline & Run-of-Show`.
2. Toggle between `Planning Roadmap` and `Day-Of Schedule`.
3. Check off a milestone and show the celebratory particle animation.
4. Click `Add Event Slot` to show custom timing additions.

---

### Scene 5: Vendor Checklists & 1-Click AI Outreach Email (2:35 – 3:05)
> **Presenter / Voiceover:**
> *"Managing vendors is where most planners get stuck. In the Vendors tab, every partner is tracked across a clear status pipeline: researching, contacted, shortlisted, and booked.
> 
> Clicking into any vendor expands an AI Vetting Checklist with specific interview questions to ask before signing contracts. Even better, click 'Generate AI Outreach Email' to produce a tailored, professional RFP with your event dates, headcount, and budget ready to copy or open directly in your mail client."*

**What to show on screen:**
1. Navigate to `Vendors & Checklists`.
2. Expand the `AI Vetting Questions` accordion on a Photography or Catering card.
3. Click `Generate AI Outreach Email`, show the formatted subject and body, and click `Copy Draft`.

---

### Scene 6: AI Idea Studio & Guest RSVP Manager (3:05 – 3:30)
> **Presenter / Voiceover:**
> *"In the AI Idea Studio, browse personalized entertainment concepts, signature cocktails, and eco-friendly favors. Each idea includes an AI explanation of why it fits your theme. Like an idea? Click '+ Add to Budget' to automatically convert it into an expense line item.
> 
> Meanwhile, the Guest List & RSVP Manager tracks party headcounts and automatically aggregates dietary restrictions—like vegan, celiac, and nut allergies—into an alert box for your caterer."*

**What to show on screen:**
1. Navigate to `AI Idea Studio`, show category chips, and click `+ Add to Budget` on an idea.
2. Click `Generate More Ideas with AI` to show dynamic brainstorming.
3. Navigate to `Guest & RSVP`, show dietary alerts box and guest table.

---

### Scene 7: PlanBot AI Chat & Master PDF Export (3:30 – 3:55)
> **Presenter / Voiceover:**
> *"Finally, meet PlanBot—your floating AI event director. Accessible anywhere in the app, PlanBot answers questions in real-time: 'How can I trim 15% from catering?', 'Write a 2-minute heartwarming toast', or 'What is our rain contingency plan?'
> 
> PlanBot answers with structured advice and actionable buttons that can directly inject items into your budget. When you're ready, click 'Print Master Plan' to generate a clean, executive PDF packet for your vendors and coordinators."*

**What to show on screen:**
1. Click the floating `Ask PlanBot` button.
2. Click the prompt chip *"How can I cut 15% from catering costs?"*.
3. Show the rich markdown reply with bullet points and action buttons.
4. Click `Print Master Plan` in the top navbar to reveal the printable layout and JSON backup download.

## 🚀 How to Run the Project Locally

```bash
# 1. Install dependencies
npm install

# 2. Start the local development server
npm run dev
# -> Opens http://localhost:5173

# 3. Build for production (100% verified for Vercel)
npm run build
```
